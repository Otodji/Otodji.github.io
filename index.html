01 · About me

Statistics at the core, data science in practice.

I am an engineer in statistics and finance, currently completing a Master 2 in Data Science (Finance track) at Université Paris-Saclay, with about three years of experience in data analysis and business intelligence in industry.

My research is in probability distribution theory: building new families of distributions, studying their properties, and applying them to data in finance, actuarial science, engineering and socio-economic development. I turn this research into open-source software used by other statisticians.

In industry, I reported directly to a CEO, built anomaly-detection algorithms and management tools, and delivered measurable results: +25% revenue on a product line and −30% receivables.
